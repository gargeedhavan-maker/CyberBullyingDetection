HOW IT WORKS

Natural Language Processing Pipeline

Social Media Comment
↓
Text Preprocessing
↓
TF-IDF Vectorization
↓
Logistic Regression
↓
Prediction

Text Preprocessing:

Cleans the entered text before classification by converting to lowercase, removing punctuation, and trimming spaces.

TF-IDF:

Converts text into numerical features by calculating term frequency and inverse document frequency across the vocabulary.

Logistic Regression:

Classifies the comment into bullying or not bullying using a linear decision boundary with the Sigmoid probability function.

Prediction:

Displays the final classification (Cyber Bullying or Not Cyber Bullying) and confidence percentage to the user.
