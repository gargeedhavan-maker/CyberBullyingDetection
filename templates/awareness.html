CYBER SAFETY AWARENESS

Online Safety Guide and Recommendations

WHAT IS CYBER BULLYING?

Cyber bullying is the use of digital communication platforms, social media, or messaging apps to harass, insult, humiliate, or threaten someone repeatedly. It can cause significant emotional and psychological distress.

WHAT SHOULD YOU DO?

  • Don't respond emotionally
  • Save evidence (take screenshots of comments and profiles)
  • Block the person immediately
  • Report the account to the platform moderators
  • Tell a trusted person, parent, or college counselor

STAY SAFE ONLINE

  • Keep your social media accounts private to control who can view your posts
  • Never share private personal information like addresses, phone numbers, or passwords
  • Think twice before posting or commenting publicly
  • Regularly review and update your privacy and security settings
